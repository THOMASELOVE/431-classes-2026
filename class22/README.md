## Some Tools to Search for Data

1. You can look up all of the data sets on CRAN at <https://observe.rstats.dev/datasets>.
2. Paperclip data sets might be a good thing or not - it's frankly unclear to me. [Learn more here](https://gxl.ai/blog/introducing-paperclip-datasets/), and [visit here](https://paperclip.gxl.ai/datasets) for more.

**Here's some advice from 432 Project A this past year...**

Some sources of data we’d like to see people use include:

1.  [Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/data_documentation/index.htm)
2.  The [Health and Retirement Study](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products/public-survey-data?_ga=2.79574685.849210420.1612760982-241136149.1612760982)
3.  The many many public use data sets available at [ICSPR](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/icpsrweb/ICPSR/)
4.  [National Center on Health Statistics](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data_access/ftp_data.htm) which includes NHANES but also other data sets.
5.  The [General Social Survey](https://gssdataexplorer.norc.org/) although the problem there is a lack of quantitative variables.
6.  A data set from the [Tidy Tuesday archive](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday) or from the [Data is Plural archive](https://dataset-finder.netlify.app/) might be a good candidate, if it meets our requirements.

- For examples of using public microdata from surveys, I recommend the book "[Analyze Survey Data for Free](http://asdfree.com/)" which has, for example, information on MEPS, the Health and Retirement Study, the General Social Survey, NHANES, the National Immunization Survey and many others.

In class projects, you are not permitted to use data from a textbook or other educational resource for learning statistics, data science or related methods (online or otherwise), with the specific exception of data from the Tidy Tuesday repository and the Data is Plural archive mentioned above. 

- [Kaggle Public Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets) aren't interesting enough or well-enough documented, usually.
- Additional repositories that are out-of-bounds for my projects include: 
    - the [Cleveland Clinic Statistical Education dataset repository](https://www.lerner.ccf.org/qhs/datasets/), 
    - the [Vanderbilt University Biostatistics Datasets](https://hbiostat.org/data/), 
    - the [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/index.php), 
- It's not a great idea to type “regression data sets” into Google - rarely does that lead to an interesting project.
