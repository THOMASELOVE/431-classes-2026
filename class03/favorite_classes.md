# Welcome to 431 Survey

I asked you "What has made your favorite university classes your favorite?"

Here is a summary (built by Google Gemini) of the main themes and insights from the individual responses[^1]. I've left this unedited[^2] in order to make a point[^3].

[^1]: I did not associate names with responses in what I let Gemini see, of course.
[^2]: Failing to edit this makes me very uncomfortable.
[^3]: The cause-and-effect language found here is not really motivated in the slightest by the raw comments from students. This amalgamation of findings leaves out some things I would have kept, and puts more emphasis on things where it finds a specific example that justifies its point than I wish it did.

### Key Drivers of a Great Class Experience

* **Engaging & Passionate Professors:** By far the most frequently cited factor. Students thrive under instructors who are enthusiastic, relatable, approachable, bring humor, and create a welcoming, low-intimidation atmosphere (e.g., easy to talk to in office hours, open to questions).
* **Interactive & Hands-on Learning:** A strong preference for active learning over passive, traditional lectures. Favorites include small group discussions, lab sessions, flipped classrooms, hands-on practice (like statistics software or a teaching kitchen), and problem-solving.
* **Practical Relevance & Real-World Application:** Students are most invested when content directly applies to their daily tasks, career goals, or real-world scenarios—such as applying chemistry to biology, using AI in healthcare, or connecting theory to clinical practice.
* **Class Community & Interpersonal Connection:** Smaller class sizes foster strong bonds with peers and instructors, making collaboration, open discussions, and joint problem-solving much easier.
* **Clear Structure & Fair Organization:** Well-planned courses with transparent grading, reasonable workloads, clear expectations, and opportunities to learn/correct mistakes without high grade anxiety are highly valued.

### Favorite Subjects & Standout Examples

* **STEM & Applied Sciences:** High marks for Bioorganic Chemistry (seeing chemistry applied to biology), Statistics labs (applying software), AI in healthcare, and Nutrition teaching kitchens.
* **Interdisciplinary & Perspective-Shifting Courses:** International Relations (introduced new ways of thinking and inspired study abroad/public health paths) and English Literature focusing on Greek mythology.
* **Favorable Logistical Factors:** Earlier class schedules (morning/early afternoon) were preferred over late evening classes, which were cited as draining due to long commutes and tight turnarounds.
